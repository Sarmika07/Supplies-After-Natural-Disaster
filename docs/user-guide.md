# Dispatcher user guide

1. Open **Overview** and confirm the timestamp and current route progress.
2. Open **New disruption**.
3. Choose a failure state: vehicle unavailable, urgent addition, cancellation, road closure or capacity shortage.
4. Click **Inject & replan**.
5. Open **Reports & KPIs** to compare ReliefRoute with the simple baseline.
6. Open **Dispatch plan** to read the decision trace and understand which stops moved.
7. Check service, commitments, distance, cost, emissions, late stops and unassigned work before approving.
8. If work is unassigned, escalate it to manual dispatch; do not treat the plan as fully recovered.
9. Use **Reset scenario** to reproduce another test from the same starting state.

The MVP is simulation-only. It does not update real vehicles or field routes.
